The world data this distribution ships
======================================

The code in this repository is MIT (see LICENSE) and the typefaces are OFL
(see LICENSE-OFL.txt). The geographic data is neither, and this file says what
it is — because two of the three sources REQUIRE attribution and none of that
may be silent.

What is shipped, and under what terms
-------------------------------------

Natural Earth — PUBLIC DOMAIN
    Country outlines, state and province outlines, and inland water.
    https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
    Natural Earth's own terms place its data in the public domain: "no
    permission is needed to use Natural Earth". The provenance is recorded
    here all the same, because the day somebody asks where a border came from
    is a day this file answers.

GeoNames — CC BY 4.0
    The names and coordinates of populated places.
    https://www.geonames.org/
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    ATTRIBUTION IS REQUIRED. It is given in four places, and the one that
    matters most is the third: a copy of the baked table can never be
    separated from its licence.
      - this file
      - the header of `assets/atlas.nix`, where the sources are declared
      - a section INSIDE the baked table itself (`world.atlas`)
      - the screen, through the text key `atlas-credits`

OpenStreetMap, via Geofabrik — ODbL
    Street names and geometry, in the per-region packs a person may choose to
    download. Nothing of this is shipped: it becomes true on a machine that
    installs a pack.
    https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
    https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/
    ATTRIBUTION IS REQUIRED, and so is sharing alike any derived database.
    The same four places carry it.

What is NOT here
----------------

Nothing personal (invariant 13). This is the world: borders, water, the names
of places, and a catalogue of regions. Where the owner has been is in the
photographs on his own disk, and it stays there.
